## 📋 Dashboard Overview

| Section | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **KPI Banner** | Total Employees (50K), Avg Income (26K), Attrition Flag (25K), Avg Salary Hike (24.62), Years in Company (11) |
| **Filters** | Income Group (High/Low/Medium), Department, JobRole |
| **Avg Hourly Rate of Research Scientists** | Bar chart by job role — all ~115–116 |
| **Attrition vs Last Year Promotion** | Bubble chart — Groups 1-5 (15.9K), 6-10 (4.8K), 11-15 (2.3K), 16+ (2.1K) |
| **Job Role vs Work-Life Balance** | Stacked bar — work-life balance scores 1–4 per job role |
| **Avg Working Years per Department** | Treemap — Software/Sales (21), Support/Hardware/HR (20) |
| **Avg Attrition Rate by Department** | Bar chart — Sales (8.5K), HR (8.4K), R&D (8.3K), etc. |
| **Attrition Rate vs Monthly Income** | Donut — High (33.4%), Low (33.37%), Medium (33.18%) |
