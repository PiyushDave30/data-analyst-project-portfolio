## 🔑 Key Insights

### Tableau — Superstore USA
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Sales | ₹2.30M |
| Profit Margin | 12.47% |
| Return Rate | 5.91% |
| Top Category | Technology (36.40%) |
| 2nd Category | Furniture (32.30%) |
| 3rd Category | Office Supplies (31.30%) |
| Top Sub-Category | Phones (14.37%) + Chairs (14.30%) |
| Top Product | Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier (61,600) |
| Top Segment | Consumer (50.56%) |
| Sales Trend | Growth from 4,520 (2014) to 1,18,448 (2018) |

**Key findings:**
- Technology leads sales at 36.40% — driven by Phones and Machines
- Consumer segment is the biggest customer group at 50.56%
- Sales show strong upward trend from 2014 to 2018
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Copier is the single top-selling product

### Power BI — Finance Dataset (2013–2014)
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Sales | 118.73M |
| Gross Sales | 1,27,932K |
| Total Profit | 16,893.7K |
| Total Discount | 9,205K |
| Top Product by Sales | Paseo (33M) |
| Top Product by Profit | Paseo (4.8M) |
| Top Segment by Sales | Government (53M) |
| Top Segment by Profit | Government (11.39M) |
| Top Country by Sales | United States (25.03M) |
| Top Country by Profit | France (3.78M) |
| Peak Sales Month | October (21.7M) |
| Peak Profit Month | October (3.4M) |
| Loss Making Segment | Enterprise (-0.61M) |

**Key findings:**
- Government segment dominates both sales (53M) and profit (11.39M)
- Enterprise segment is the only loss-making segment at -0.61M
- Paseo is the top product in both sales and profit
- October is consistently the best month for both sales and profit
- France leads in profit despite USA leading in sales — higher European margins
