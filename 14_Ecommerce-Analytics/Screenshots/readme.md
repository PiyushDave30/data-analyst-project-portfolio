# 🖼️ Dashboard Screenshots

This folder contains full-page Screenshots of all 6 pages of the Ecommerce Analytics Dashboard. Each screenshot is described below along with the business question it answers.

📁 **Location:** `/Screenshots`

---

## 1. Home

![Home](1.%20Home.png)

🔗 [Open full image](1.%20Home.png)

The landing/navigation page for the report. Presents the six analysis sections (Executive Summary, Revenue & Profit Analysis, Customer Insights, Return Order Insights, Marketing & Seasonal Analysis, Conclusion) as clickable cards, giving stakeholders a guided entry point into the dashboard rather than a raw wall of charts.

**Purpose:** Navigation and first impression — sets context for what the report covers before diving into numbers.

---

## 2. Executive Overview

![Executive Overview](2.%20Executive%20Overview.png)

🔗 [Open full image](2.%20Executive%20Overview.png)

A single-page company health check. Surfaces the six KPIs leadership cares about most — **Total Revenue (236.76K)**, **Total Profit (66.19K)**, **Total Orders (1,000)**, **Total Customers (326)**, **Profit Margin % (27.96%)**, and **Return Rate % (8.20%)** — alongside a monthly revenue/profit trend line, revenue split by country and category, a region-level revenue/profit bar chart, and a sortable country/city performance table.

**Business question answered:** *"How is the business performing overall, and where geographically is that performance concentrated?"*

---

## 3. Revenue & Profit Analysis

![Revenue & Profit Analysis](3.%20Revenue%20&%20Profit%20Analysis.png)

🔗 [Open full image](3.%20Revenue%20&%20Profit%20Analysis.png)

Drills from company-wide KPIs into what's actually driving (or eroding) profit. Includes a field-parameter toggle to switch every visual between Total Revenue and Total Profit view, a sub-category profit ranking, category-level profit donut, payment method breakdown, top 5 and bottom 5 products by profit, and a discount-bucket chart showing profit turning negative once discounts exceed 20%.

**Business question answered:** *"Which categories and products drive revenue vs. profit, and where is discounting causing orders to turn negative?"*

---

## 4. Customer Analysis

![Customer Analysis](4.%20Customer%20Analysis.png)

🔗 [Open full image](4.%20Customer%20Analysis.png)

Segments the customer base to separate one-time transactions from durable, repeat-driven revenue. Shows customers by payment method, customer type (Repeat: 275 vs. One-time: 51), customer segment (Consumer / Corporate / Home Office), average order value by segment, a revenue-by-customer-type donut (95.2% from repeat customers), and a ranked top-customers table.

**Business question answered:** *"Who are the highest-value customers, and how much revenue comes from repeat buyers vs. one-time buyers?"*

---

## 5. Return Orders Insight

![Return Orders Insight](5.%20Return%20Orders.png)

🔗 [Open full image](5.%20Return%20Orders.png)

Quantifies the cost of returns and isolates where they're concentrated. Headline metrics include **82 Return Orders**, an **8.20% Return Rate**, **18.37K in Lost Revenue**, and **5.12K in Lost Profit**, supported by return rate % by category (Fashion highest at 14.16%), profit vs. loss-profit by category, a monthly orders-vs-returns trend, and a shipping mode performance table comparing Same-Day, Standard, and Express on return rate, delivery days, shipping cost, and profit margin.

**Business question answered:** *"What's driving returns, and which categories or shipping modes hurt net profit once returns are factored in?"*

---

## 6. Marketing & Seasonal Analysis

![Marketing & Seasonal Analysis](6.%20Marketing%20&%20Seasonal%20Insights.png)

🔗 [Open full image](6.%20Marketing%20&%20Seasonal%20Insights.png)

Connects acquisition spend to outcomes and surfaces seasonal demand patterns. Shows marketing channel by revenue and profit (Organic Search leads), average order value by channel and segment, weekend vs. weekday revenue/profit comparison, a quarterly revenue trend by channel, and marketing channel by customer count.

**Business question answered:** *"Which marketing channel acquires the most valuable orders, and how should Q4 seasonality shape inventory and spend?"*
