## 📋 Dashboard Pages

| Page | Key Visuals |
|------|------------|
| **Home / Overview** | KPI cards — Total Sales ($29.4M), Profit ($12.1M), Orders (60K), Customers (18K), Profit % (41.15%), Avg Order Value ($486.09) |
| **Sales Analysis** | Sales by Year, Month, Quarter, Country, Gender, Weekday/Weekend |
| **Profit Analysis** | Profit by Year, Month, Quarter, Country, Gender |
| **Orders Analysis** | Orders by Year, Month, Quarter, Country, Gender, Unique Customers |

---

## 🛠️ Power BI Features Used

- **Data Modelling** — Star schema with fact and dimension tables
- **DAX Measures** — Custom KPIs for Profit %, Avg Order Value, Total Profit
- **Slicers** — Region and Product filters across all pages
- **Navigation Buttons** — Page-to-page navigation (Sales ↔ Profit)
- **Visualizations** — Bar charts, line charts, pie charts, KPI cards

---

## 🔗 How to Open
1. Download `Adventure_Works.pbix`
2. Open with **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** (free download from Microsoft)
3. All data is embedded — no external connections needed
